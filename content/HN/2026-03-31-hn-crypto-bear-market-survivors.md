# HN: Crypto Winter Survival - Which Projects Actually Built

The HN community reflected on the 2024-2025 market downturn and identified which crypto projects emerged stronger through actual development.

## Market Context

The crypto market saw significant correction:

| Metric | Peak (Late 2024) | Trough (Mid 2025) | Current |
|--------|------------------|-------------------|---------|
| BTC Price | $142K | $52K | $89K |
| Total DeFi TVL | $340B | $89B | $180B |
| Active Developers | 45K | 28K | 38K |
| Active Protocols | 12,500 | 6,200 | 8,400 |

## Projects That Survived (And Why)

### Uniswap - Protocol Maturity
- Volume remained even as prices dropped
- V4 hooks attracted developer interest
- Revenue held at $150M+ annually

### Ethereum - Ecosystem Strength
- Layer 2 adoption accelerated
- Staking participation increased
- Developer tooling matured

### Aave - Institutional Trust
- Government entities became depositors
- Risk models proved robust
- Revenue diversified across assets

### Solana - Technical Resilience
- Network uptime improved dramatically
- Institutional interest grew
- Ecosystem projects consolidated

## What the Bear Market Revealed

### Technical Merit vs Token Hype

| Project Type | Survival Rate | Key Factor |
|--------------|---------------|------------|
| Pure DeFi | 45% | Real revenue |
| NFT projects | 8% | Speculation collapse |
| L1/L2 chains | 35% | Developer ecosystem |
| Gaming/Metaverse | 12% | User adoption |
| Infrastructure | 62% | Clear utility |

### HN "Who Built" Winners

1. **Real用过 protocols**: Aave, Uniswap, Chainlink
2. **Infrastructure providers**: The Graph, IPFS/Filecoin
3. **Developer tools**: Hardhat, Foundry, ethers.js
4. **Privacy solutions**: Aztec, Railgun

## Bear Market Innovations

Despite reduced prices, development continued:

| Innovation | Description | Status |
|------------|-------------|--------|
| ZK-rollups | Privacy-preserving scaling | Production |
| Account abstraction | UX improvements (ERC-4337) | Adoption growing |
| Intent-based UX | User intent replacing transactions | Early adoption |
| Restaking | EigenLayer's validation economy | $15B TVL |

## Lessons for Next Cycle

HN wisdom on crypto building:

> "Build during bear markets, ship during bull markets"

> "Token price is noise. User count and revenue are signal."

> "The best projects in 2025 were invisible in 2024"
