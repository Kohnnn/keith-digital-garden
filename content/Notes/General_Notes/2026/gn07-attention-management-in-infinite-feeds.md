# gn07 attention management in infinite feeds

Every social media platform, news site, and information service is optimized for one metric: time spent. Not value delivered, not insight per minute, not quality of decision-making. Attention is the raw material of the attention economy, and infinite feeds are extraction machines designed to capture as much of it as possible. Fighting them with willpower is a losing game — the system is designed to defeat individual discipline.

## key idea
Structural interventions beat behavioral ones. Removing the feed entirely is more effective than trying to moderate consumption within it. The people who manage attention best do not have stronger willpower — they have better environments.

## notes
- Infinite feeds exploit variable reward scheduling — the same mechanism as slot machines
- Context switching between information sources costs 20-40% of productive time
- The default mode network requires idle periods to consolidate learning — constant input prevents consolidation
- My approach: batch information consumption into specific time blocks, not continuous grazing
- Curated sources (RSS, newsletters) outperform algorithmic feeds for signal quality
