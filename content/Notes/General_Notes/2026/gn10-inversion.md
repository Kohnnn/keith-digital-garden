# gn10 inversion

Inversion is the mental model of solving problems backward. Instead of asking "how do I succeed?", ask "how do I guarantee failure?" The answers to the second question are often clearer and more actionable than the first. Once you know what to avoid, the path to success becomes easier to find.

## key idea
The Stoics used this as premeditatio malorum — the premeditation of evils. Imagine everything that could go wrong, then prepare for it. The exercise does not make you pessimistic. It makes you resilient.

## in practice
- In trading: instead of "how do I make money?" ask "what trades would lose money?" and avoid them
- In writing: instead of "how do I write well?" ask "what makes writing unreadable?" and avoid those patterns
- In investing: instead of "what stock will go up?" ask "what companies will fail?" and check if yours is on the list
