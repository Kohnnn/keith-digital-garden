# NVIDIA Computex 2026: Nemotron 3 Ultra, RTX Spark, and the AI Platform Era

Jensen Huang opened Computex 2026 in Taipei with a clear message: NVIDIA is no longer a chip company — it's a full-stack AI platform. The keynote spanned hardware, models, physical AI, and a new PC form factor.

---

## Nemotron 3 Ultra: The Open-Weight Leader

Nemotron 3 Ultra (550B parameters, 55B active via MoE) tops the US open-weights rankings with an Intelligence Index of 48, delivering 300+ tokens/second inference. Optimized for multi-step task execution with minimal oversight. Over 50M downloads, 2,500+ enterprise builders.

## RTX Spark: AI on Your Desk

New chip category putting AI acceleration directly into laptops and desktops. Renders 90GB 3D scenes, edits 12K video, generates 4K AI video, plays AAA games at 1440p 100+ FPS with DLSS 4.5. Adobe rebuilding Photoshop and Premiere for RTX Spark.

## Cosmos 3: Physical AI Platform

The Physical AI Omnimodel handling perception, simulation, and control in one framework. Robotics, autonomous vehicles, industrial automation. NVIDIA's bet that the next frontier is the physical world.

## Vera CPU + DGX Station

Custom ARM silicon (Vera) plus desktop supercomputer (DGX Station). Rubin CPX for datacenter. Full vertical integration: silicon → system → model → software.
