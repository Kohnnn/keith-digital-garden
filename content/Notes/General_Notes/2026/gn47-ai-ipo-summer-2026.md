# AI IPO Summer 2026: Anthropic, Cursor, and the $160 Billion Public Market Wave

June 2026 will be remembered as the month AI went public. Three companies with a combined paper value north of $1 trillion announced public market intentions within two weeks.

---

## Anthropic Files Confidentially at $965B

On June 2, 2026, Anthropic filed its confidential S-1, just four days after closing a $65B Series H at $965B post-money — the largest AI startup valuation ever, surpassing OpenAI's $852B. Q2 2026 revenue projected at $10.9B, annualized run-rate ~$47B. Target: October 2026 on NASDAQ.

Enterprise API adoption scaling across banking, healthcare, legal. Multi-year contracts. AWS Bedrock + Google Cloud partnerships embedding Claude as infrastructure-level AI. 134M MAU. Profitability target: 2028.

## SpaceX IPO + Cursor Acquisition

SpaceX listed on Nasdaq June 12 at $135/share, closing at $192.46 — the largest IPO in history. Four days later on June 16, SpaceX exercised a $60B all-stock option to acquire Anysphere (Cursor), the largest venture-backed acquisition ever.

Cursor's revenue: $100M ARR (Jan 2025) → $500M (Jun 2025) → $1B (Nov 2025) → $2B (Feb 2026) → ~$4B (Jun 2026). 60%+ of Fortune 500 using Cursor. ~15x revenue multiple.

## Competitive Reordering

Anthropic ($965B) > OpenAI ($852B) > SpaceX ($1.85T post-IPO). OpenAI's Q4 2026 IPO is preparing. Claude Code overtook Cursor and Copilot in developer surveys, but the SpaceX acquisition gives Cursor a strategic backstop.

Goldman Sachs: 2026 US IPO proceeds could hit $160B — 4x 2025 levels.
