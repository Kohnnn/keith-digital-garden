# Bitcoin Crash June 2026: A Macro-Driven Correction

The crypto market experienced its most severe correction of 2026 in early June, with Bitcoin plummeting below $63,000 and briefly touching $61,165 — a 30% decline year-to-date. Unlike 2022 (Terra/Luna, FTX), this is a macro-driven event.

---

## The Four Forces

**1. Hawkish Fed** — Elevated rates despite market expectations of cuts. Sticky inflation forced the FOMC to hold firm, squeezing all risk assets.

**2. Geopolitical Risk** — US-Iran tensions added risk premium. Bitcoin's correlation with traditional risk assets became starkly visible.

**3. Strategy (MSTR) Sells Bitcoin** — First time Saylor's company broke its "never sell" vow. Psychological damage from the most committed institutional holder selling triggered panic.

**4. Record ETF Outflows** — Longest streak of Bitcoin ETF outflows ever. Institutional buyers pulled back, stripping liquidity.

## The Numbers

Bitcoin fell ~48% from October 2025 highs. $1.8B leveraged positions liquidated in 24h. Total crypto market cap contracted $800B+ since June 1. Ethereum down ~35%. Altcoins down 50%+.

## Why This Is Different

Macro-driven, not crypto-specific. Recovery depends on Fed policy, not crypto developments. $65K floor broke with little resistance. Thin liquidity means sharp moves either way.

## What to Watch

ETF outflows stabilize first. Then: Strategy resuming accumulation, ETF flow reversal, dovish FOMC. Previous macro-driven BTC drawdowns of this magnitude have historically been buying opportunities.
