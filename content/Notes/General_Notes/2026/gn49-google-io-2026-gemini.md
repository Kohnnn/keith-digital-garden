# Google I/O 2026: Gemini 3.5, Android 17, and the Search Redesign

Google I/O 2026 was its most aggressive product cycle in years. Theme: AI is the platform, everything else is an interface.

---

## Gemini 3.5: The Tiered Model Strategy

**Gemini 3.5 Flash** — Workhorse shipping in AI Mode Search on day one. First time Google ships a Gemini model in Search at launch. Computer Use tool in public preview: browser, mobile, desktop actions.

**Gemini 3.5 Pro** — Limited Vertex preview since May 19. 2M-token context, Deep Think reasoning. Replaces Ultra in the lineup.

**Gemini Spark** — Entry-level on-device model, priced aggressively against open-weights.

## AI Mode Search

Biggest Search redesign in 25 years. AI Mode replaces traditional results with agentic reasoning — ask, AI retrieves and synthesizes — with fallback to traditional search for factuality verification. Simple queries get traditional results; complex ones get agentic. Google walks the line between ad business cannibalization and existential threat from ChatGPT/Perplexity.

## Android 17

Gemini replaces Google Assistant as default. On-device Spark for offline tasks, system-level agent permissions, AI notification triage, Context Cards API.

## Android XR Glasses

Built with Samsung and Qualcomm. Developer kits Q3 2026. Gemini overlays on the physical world: real-time translation, navigation, contextual information — no headset bulk.
