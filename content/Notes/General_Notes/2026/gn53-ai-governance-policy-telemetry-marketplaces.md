# Policy Telemetry Marketplaces: The New AI Compliance Layer for Regulated Sectors

The 2026 AI market is moving toward one painful truth: compliance can’t be a checklist anymore. Regulated sectors are pushing for standardized policy telemetry marketplaces where AI behavior is continuously measured, versioned, and exposed to auditors as product.

---

## Why this is changing now

Financial services, healthcare, and law now run AI at enterprise scale. Traditional governance (quarterly audit, manual controls, ad-hoc logs) failed to keep pace. The answer has become standardized telemetry: who called which model, what prompt, which policy constraints were violated, and what override happened.

Marketplaces are the distribution layer: vendors publish policy schemas, evaluation signals, and evidence packs that can be subscribed to across enterprises.

## What these marketplaces include

- **Decision metadata**: input features, policy checks, and confidence thresholds.
- **Runtime traces**: tool calls, tool outcomes, retry loops, and fallback events.
- **Post-incident artifacts**: root-cause tags, remediation commits, rollback IDs.
- **Governance fingerprints**: model version, provider, deployment region, and policy bundle hash.

The core value is interoperability: one control signal can serve multiple enterprises if policy syntax is shared.

## What to watch

The first winners are the vendors who reduce audit cost by 50%+ while increasing time-to-detect for high-risk anomalies. The losers are AI stack providers that expose raw logs but no policy-level meaning.

Buy a vendor by asking 3 questions: Is telemetry auditable end-to-end? Can customers prove it to regulators in 24 hours? Can policy changes be hot-patched without downtime?
