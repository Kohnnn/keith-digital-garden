# AI Infrastructure Capex and the Power Grid Constraint

$725B AI spending is no longer a budget line; it’s a power-grid planning problem.

---

## The arithmetic

AI capex in 2026 tracks three synchronized curves: model scale, inferencing demand, and electricity costs. Datacenter builds can’t wait for “long-term power planning” anymore. They’re racing one-to-three year permitting cycles where power reserve is the bottleneck.

Big Tech is committing to hyperscale AI with urgency, but each GW added requires local permitting, cooling contracts, and grid interconnection risk.

## The structural shift

Infrastructure teams now optimize differently:
- Load location near stable renewables and transmission corridors.
- Co-locate inference near user demand to reduce bandwidth costs.
- Prioritize inference efficiency over raw model size.

Layoffs in support engineering and spending on platform SREs are correlated: fewer people manually patching incidents, more money in automation and predictive maintenance.

## Strategic implication

AI leaders are quietly becoming energy strategists. If a firm can’t secure clean, predictable electricity, it loses the ability to deploy new models on schedule.
