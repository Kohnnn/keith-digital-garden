# AI Is Slowing Down: The Structural Plateau

Multiple signals in June 2026 point to the same conclusion: the AI investment cycle is hitting diminishing returns on marginal output. Not a crash — a plateau.

---

## The evidence

- **Model improvements are narrowing.** Each new frontier model shows smaller gains over the previous. The low-hanging fruit (scaling laws, RLVR, data quality) is picked.
- **Infrastructure spending is decoupling from revenue.** Big Tech’s $630B+ AI splurge is not matched by proportional revenue growth. The ratio of capex to AI-driven revenue is widening.
- **Consumer adoption is flat.** “Not everyone is using AI for everything” — the viral adoption curve of 2023–2025 is over. AI usage is now a normal utility, not a growth story.
- **Enterprise pilots are stalling.** Many enterprises started AI pilots in 2024–2025 but haven’t scaled them. The gap between pilot and production is wider than expected.

## What this means

Plateaus are not crashes. They’re consolidation phases. The winners are the companies that can survive at current spending levels until the next breakthrough (agentic workflows, physical AI, reasoning at scale). The losers are the ones that bet on continued exponential growth in model quality.

## The strategic response

- Shift from “model quality” to “reliability at current quality”
- Invest in infrastructure that works on today’s models, not tomorrow’s
- Prepare for a 12–24 month period where the best model is the one you can run, not the one you can imagine
