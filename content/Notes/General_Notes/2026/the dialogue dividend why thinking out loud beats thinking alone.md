Jakub Skoczen on The Signalist: why a 5-min hallway conversation solves what a week of solo thinking couldn't. The structure of dialogue forces precision that internal monologue never requires — speaking demands sentences with claims, listeners give real-time feedback (frowns, questions, nods).

Draws on Mercier & Sperber (reasoning evolved for social argumentation, not solitary truth), Vygotsky's ZPD (conversation pushes you above your solo ceiling), Clark & Chalmers' extended mind (the other person is part of the cognitive system, not an external resource).

**The dialogue dividend** = compound returns from thinking in conversation with trusted people. Invisible until you try to collect it and realize you never invested.

Flags two risks: (1) remote/async cultures systematically remove conditions for informal conversation, eroding cognitive infrastructure silently. (2) LLMs as thinking partners give you half — the precision-forcing — but not the critical disagreement. Models are sycophantic by default; you have to ask them to disagree, and even then they eventually conform.
