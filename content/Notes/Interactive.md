## Explore by theme

### Social systems

Simulations that make trust, segregation, feedback loops, and voting tangible. Open them, drag the variables, and watch the equilibrium shift.

- [[Notes/Interactive/The-Evolution-of-Trust|The Evolution of Trust]] — repeated-game cooperation, noise, and repair.
- [[Notes/Interactive/Parable-of-the-Polygons|Parable of the Polygons]] — small biases, tipping points, and emergent segregation.
- [[Notes/Interactive/To-Build-a-Better-Ballot|To Build a Better Ballot]] — voting systems with draggable electorates and counting flows.
- [[Notes/Interactive/LOOPY|LOOPY]] — draw, tune, and simulate feedback systems.

### Model intuition

Hands-on data-splitting, bias-variance tradeoffs, and probability drills that build ML intuition before the equations.

- [[Notes/Interactive/Train-Test-and-Validation-Sets|Train, Test, and Validation Sets]] — drag examples, update boundaries, feel why the test set stays untouched.
- [[Notes/Interactive/The-Bias-Variance-Tradeoff|The Bias-Variance Tradeoff]] — underfitting vs. overfitting as a model-complexity knob.
- [[Notes/Interactive/ROC-AUC|ROC & AUC]] — threshold sweeps and AUC interpretation in one interactive flow.
- [[Notes/Interactive/Principal-Component-Analysis|Principal Component Analysis]] — rotate your attention, not just your axes.

### Math you can touch

Geometry, probability, and physics sandboxes that turn abstract ideas into interactive play.

- [[Notes/Interactive/Tesseract|Tesseract]] — 4D geometry built from projections, rotations, and intersections.
- [[Notes/Interactive/Sound|Sound]] — acoustics from particle motion to superposition and synthesis.
- [[Notes/Interactive/Rigid-Body-Collisions|Rigid Body Collisions]] — 2D collision resolution with vector geometry and normals.

### Cryptography and proof playgrounds

Hashing, signatures, and zero-knowledge proofs explored as interactive labs rather than slides.

- [[Notes/Interactive/Blockchain-101-Combined-Flow|Blockchain 101 Combined Flow]] — the best external sequence through hashing, signatures, and privacy.
- [[Notes/Interactive/Blockchain|Blockchain]] — chained state, nonce updates, and the tamper cascade.
- [[Notes/Interactive/Public-Private-Keys|Public / Private Keys]] — key generation, signing, and verification in a browser.
- [[Notes/Interactive/Zero-Knowledge-Proof-Demo|Zero-Knowledge Proof Demo]] — proof without disclosure via a selectable US map.
