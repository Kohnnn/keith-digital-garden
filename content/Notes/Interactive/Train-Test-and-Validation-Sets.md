## At a glance

<div class="casefile-summary">
  <div>
    <span class="casefile-label">Why this matters</span>
    <p>Choosing splits is usually taught as a rule of thumb. This makes the mechanics physical: drag data, switch features, and watch how each split changes the model you trust.</p>
  </div>
  <div>
    <span class="casefile-label">What to try first</span>
    <p>Jump to the model scene and move the boundary. Switch features, then scroll into the validation and test sections to see why that interaction belongs in the middle of the workflow rather than at the end.</p>
  </div>
  <div>
    <span class="casefile-label">Time and level</span>
    <p>About 10 to 15 minutes. Beginner friendly. One of the easiest machine-learning routes to start with because the workflow logic is visible immediately.</p>
  </div>
