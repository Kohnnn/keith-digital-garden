# CLARITY Act clears Senate Banking Committee — stablecoin yield fight continues

The Senate Banking Committee voted 15-9 to advance the CLARITY Act to the full Senate, with Democrats Ruben Gallego and Angela Alsobrooks crossing the aisle in a last-minute deal brokered by Chairman Tim Scott. The bill merges with the Agriculture Committee version before advancing, with the White House targeting July 4 for passage ([Crypto-Corner](https://crypto-corner.com/2026/05/20/market-recap-may-2026/)).

## scene cut
I've been tracking this bill since the first draft. The 15-9 vote with bipartisan crossover tells me this has real momentum. Stablecoin yield restrictions and the ethics provision for government officials are the two unresolved threads that could still trip it up.

## signal braid
- Senate Banking Committee passed 15-9 with two Democrats crossing party lines
- Bill includes: registration rules for trading venues, SEC/CFTC jurisdictional split, stablecoin yield restrictions, legal protections for DeFi developers
- Ethics provision barring government officials from crypto ties remains unresolved
- Merges with Agriculture Committee version before floor vote
- White House targeting July 4 passage
- Industry groups called it "defining moment for American leadership"
- Banking trade associations want stablecoin yield language strengthened

## my take
The CLARITY Act passing would be the most significant US crypto legislation ever. The stablecoin yield fight is the key — banks want to restrict it, crypto firms want to preserve it. The ethics provision is a wildcard. Either way, the bill has more momentum than any crypto legislation in US history.
