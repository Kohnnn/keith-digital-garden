# Tech Layoffs 2026: 134K Cuts While Big Tech Pours $725B Into AI

Through June 2026, 212 layoff events have impacted 134,603 workers across tech. Meanwhile, Big Tech is spending $725B on AI infrastructure. The divergence tells a story about where the industry thinks value is moving.

---

## The Numbers

134,603 workers laid off across 212 events. The cuts span all segments: big tech (Google, Microsoft, Meta continuing headcount discipline), mid-market SaaS (Postmates ended no-layoff policy), and startups. The skills most affected: middle-management, QA, design, and non-core engineering.

## Where the Money Is Going

The $725B AI capex number covers: NVIDIA GPU purchases, datacenter construction, power infrastructure, and AI model training. Microsoft alone committed $80B+ for AI datacenters in FY2026. Google's Alphabet announced $80B in AI funding plans at I/O. Meta's AI capex continues rising.

The math is brutal: one $30K H100 GPU replaces 5-10 engineer-years of productivity gains. Companies aren't being cruel — they're allocating capital to where returns are highest.

## The Skills Shift

The layoffs aren't random. Companies are rebalancing from "maintenance engineering" toward "AI infrastructure engineering." The skills in demand: ML ops, GPU cluster management, inference optimization, agent deployment. The skills being cut: boilerplate CRUD development, manual QA, UI-only design.

For software engineers, the message is clear: learn to build with AI or be built by AI.
