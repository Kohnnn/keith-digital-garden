# Humanoid Robot IPO Wave: China's Robotics Companies Rush to Public Markets

More than ten Chinese robotics enterprises are preparing to go public in 2026. Unitree leads the pack with a record-breaking 132-day IPO tutoring completion.

---

## The Leaders

**Unitree Robotics** — Completed IPO tutoring in 132 days, the fastest ever. The company that showed humanoids dancing at CES and during Shanghai's Spring Festival is now the most anticipated robotics IPO in China. Backed by major Chinese VCs and strategic investors.

**Leju Robotics** — Secured nearly ¥1.5B in early funding. Officially started listing preparations. Backed by Ant Lingbo and national champions. Focus on industrial and service humanoids.

**Zhongqing Robotics** — Completed share reform. Positioned in heavy industrial robotics.

**Xinghaitu** — Also completed share reform. Focused on specialized embodied AI applications.

## Why Now

Three factors driving the rush: (1) The sector crossed $500M in global sales revenue for the first time in 2025 — there's actual revenue to show investors. (2) Unitree's public dancing demonstrations created massive retail investor awareness. (3) Chinese regulators are encouraging hard-tech IPOs, fast-tracking robotics companies.

## The Risk

First-tier companies (Unitree, Leju, Zhiyuan) have cumulative financing over ¥1.5B each and are accelerating toward public markets. But tier 2 players face narrowing funding windows. The IPO wave will separate real businesses from hype — a few will trade well, many will struggle post-listing.
