# AI Startup Funding June 2026: 395 Deals, $337B Raised

June 2026 saw 395 AI-related funding deals totaling $337B — a pace that would make 2026 the largest year for AI investment in history by a wide margin.

---

## The Breakdown

The $337B figure spans: Anthropic's $65B Series H (largest single AI round ever), OpenAI's continued capital raises, infrastructure deals (datacenter financing, GPU collateralized loans), and hundreds of smaller rounds across agents, robotics, and enterprise AI.

The composition has shifted: infrastructure now dominates deal value (datacenters, energy, chips), but agent startups lead in deal count.

## What $337B Buys

- ~2 million H100-equivalent GPUs
- Roughly 15 new gigawatt-scale datacenters
- 395 companies funded, most of which won't exist in 5 years
- The largest concentration of capital in technology history outside of public markets

## The Velocity Signal

Deal velocity increased every quarter since Q3 2025. The average time between Series A and B shrank to 11 months (was 18-24 months pre-AI). Top-tier AI startups are moving faster than any previous software cohort.

The risk: compressed timelines mean less time to find product-market fit before being pushed to scale. Many well-funded companies will flame out spectacularly.

## Where the Money Goes

- **Infrastructure** (40%) — GPUs, datacenters, energy. Capital-intensive, winner-take-most.
- **Foundation models** (25%) — Anthropic, OpenAI, Mistral, DeepSeek. Betting on the model layer.
- **Agents** (20%) — Hundreds of startups. Fragmented, competitive, high failure rate.
- **Enterprise AI** (10%) — Vertical applications in healthcare, legal, finance.
- **Robotics** (5%) — Physical AI, humanoids, industrial automation.
