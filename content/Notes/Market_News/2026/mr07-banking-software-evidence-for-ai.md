# Banking Software Buyers Demand Evidence for AI Decisions

Banking procurement changed in 2026: RFPs now require AI evidence, not feature demos.

---

## What changed

Buyers no longer ask "can it generate a credit note." They ask:
- Can you show per-decision traceability?
- Can we replay an adverse action?
- What is your model version and override policy?
- What is mean-time-to-detect on policy drift?

## Why software teams are losing

Teams built for model performance are now losing to teams with evidence systems. The buyer can compare two vendors quickly when the evidence package is machine-readable.

## Procurement pattern

Winning proposals include: incident response playbook, audit-ready telemetry, rollback path, and legal language for decision support vs final authority. In banking, explainability is no longer a legal accessory. It is the product.
