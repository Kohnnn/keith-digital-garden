# Vietnam proposes crypto as SME loan collateral in regulatory first

Vietnam's Ministry of Finance proposed letting small and medium-sized enterprises pledge digital assets, virtual assets, and intellectual property as collateral for bank loans. SMEs make up over 98% of Vietnamese businesses but receive only around 20% of total bank credit. A regulated crypto market could launch as early as Q3 2026 ([YEX](https://blog.yex.io/markets/market-recap-markets/crypto-market-weekly-recap-june-1-2026/)).

## scene cut
This is the kind of slow-build structural catalyst that most traders miss because it doesn't move price in a day. Vietnam ranks fourth in global crypto adoption. Formalizing crypto as collateral for 98% of its business ecosystem is not experimental — it's infrastructure.

## signal braid
- Ministry of Finance proposed digital assets as collateral for SME bank loans
- SMEs are 98%+ of Vietnamese businesses, receive ~20% of bank credit
- Vietnam ranks #4 globally in crypto adoption (Chainalysis)
- Regulated crypto market targeted for Q3 2026
- IP and virtual assets also included as eligible collateral types
- Part of broader reform to close SME credit gap

## my take
Sovereign adoption stories like this don't get priced in immediately. They compound. When the fourth-largest crypto adoption market formally recognizes digital assets as bankable collateral, it creates a lending flywheel that brings more capital into the system. I'm watching for follow-on moves from other ASEAN countries.
