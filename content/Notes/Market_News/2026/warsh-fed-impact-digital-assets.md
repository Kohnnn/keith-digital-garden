# Kevin Warsh Fed impact on digital assets — no cuts 2026

The Senate confirmed Kevin Warsh as Federal Reserve Chair in a 51-45 vote, replacing Jerome Powell. Warsh immediately signaled no rate cuts in 2026 — a sharp departure from market expectations that had been pricing in at least one cut by December. Core PCE hit a three-year high. The futures market began pricing potential HIKES in H2 2026 (Crypto-Corner, Verdence).

## scene cut
The Warsh confirmation is the most important macro event for crypto since the ETF approvals. A Fed chair explicitly ruling out cuts while inflation is at a three-year high changes the entire risk backdrop for digital assets. Rate-sensitive assets suffer when the carry trade shifts to cash.

## signal braid
- Senate confirmed Warsh 51-45; sworn in immediately
- He signaled no rate cuts in 2026 — futures market repriced to potential hikes
- Core PCE YoY rose at fastest pace in three years
- Consumer confidence hit record low (Conference Board)
- Real disposable income declined three consecutive months
- Personal savings rate at lowest since 2022
- BTC dropped 6% week of confirmation from $82K to $76.6K
- Spot BTC ETFs shed $1.5B in two weeks following the signal
- DOJ dropped criminal investigation into Powell prior to transition

## my take
Warsh is the right chair for the inflation environment but the wrong one for risk assets. His mandate is price stability, not asset prices. For crypto, the Warsh regime means no liquidity-driven rallies in 2026 — any BTC advance will have to be fundamental (adoption, regulation, tokenization) rather than macro (rate cuts, liquidity expansion). This is a regime change that markets are still pricing in.
