# rd01 tokenization infrastructure evidence review

The institutional tokenization push reached critical mass in spring 2026. This review consolidates the evidence across five independent vectors: SEC regulatory framework, DTCC settlement infrastructure, JPMorgan on-chain funds, Moody's ratings, and Nasdaq approval.

## evidence summary

**Regulatory (SEC):** The SEC is preparing an innovation exemption for trading platforms offering tokenized stocks under a lighter structure. Formal release expected as early as June 2026.

**Settlement (DTCC):** First limited tokenized asset trades scheduled July 2026, full rollout October. DTCC processes the bulk of US securities settlement — this is the most significant infrastructure milestone.

**Funds (JPMorgan):** Filed for SEC approval of a blockchain-based money market fund investing in short-term US Treasuries on Ethereum. This is the first major bank to file for an on-chain fund structure.

**Ratings (Moody's):** Assigned AAA rating to tokenized money market funds from Fidelity and BlackRock — a first for the asset class.

**Exchange (Nasdaq):** Received SEC approval for tokenized securities plan in March 2026.

## key finding
The five vectors are mutually reinforcing. Regulatory clarity enables exchange participation, which enables settlement infrastructure, which enables fund products, which enables ratings. The convergence of all five in a single quarter suggests tokenization is entering an adoption S-curve rather than linear growth.
