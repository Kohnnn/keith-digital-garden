# rd03 stablecoin yield landscape and regulatory crosscurrents

The stablecoin yield landscape is caught in a three-way tension: CLARITY Act restrictions limiting on-chain yield products, Hyperliquid restructuring the economics of stablecoin yield for DeFi, and institutional tokenized money market funds (Moody's AAA) competing for the same capital with lower risk profiles.

## evidence summary

**Regulatory:** CLARITY Act includes stablecoin yield restrictions that limit what platforms can offer. Banks want tighter language. Crypto firms want the restrictions removed. The stablecoin yield provision is the last major unresolved issue before the floor vote.

**DeFi:** Hyperliquid reached an agreement restructuring stablecoin yield economics, creating a new model for how DeFi protocols can offer yield products without violating regulatory constraints. Raises questions about whether DeFi yield can survive regulatory scrutiny.

**Institutional:** Moody's assigned AAA to tokenized money market funds from Fidelity and BlackRock. These offer similar yield profiles to DeFi stablecoin products but with institutional custody, audit trails, and regulatory compliance. They compete directly for the same capital.

## key finding
Stablecoin yield is being bifurcated. Institutional capital will flow toward regulated AAA-rated products. DeFi-native yield will survive but at smaller scale and with higher risk tolerance. The CLARITY Act outcome determines whether both tracks coexist or one dominates.
