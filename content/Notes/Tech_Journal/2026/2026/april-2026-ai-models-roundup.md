## context + claim

April 2026 marked a landmark month in AI. Four major labs released significant models within weeks of each other, with notable advances in context windows, multimodal processing, and architectural innovations like multi-agent systems.

## constraint map

**GPT-5.4** (OpenAI)
- Significantly extended token context window
- Improved reasoning capabilities
- Ideal for complex analytical tasks

**Claude Mythos** (Anthropic)
- Most advanced conversational model to date
- Emphasis on ethical AI interactions
- Breaking: ability to find exploits across major OSes and browsers

**Gemini 3.1 Pro** (Google DeepMind)
- Enhanced multimodal capabilities
- Native text, image, and video analysis
- Strong enterprise positioning

**Llama 4** (Meta AI)
- Focus on open-source accessibility
- Democratizing high-performance AI tools
- Community fine-tuning support

**Grok 4.20** (xAI)
- Multi-agent systems architecture
- AI models working collaboratively
- Novel approach to complex problem-solving

## my take

The context window arms race continues (GPT-5.4). Claude Mythos dominates on capability but stays closed. Llama 4 keeps pushing open-source. Grok's multi-agent approach is the architectural wild card — if it works, it's a different paradigm entirely.
