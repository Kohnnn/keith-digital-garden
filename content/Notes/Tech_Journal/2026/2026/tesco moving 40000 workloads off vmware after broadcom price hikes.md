Tesco is migrating 40,000 server workloads off VMware after Broadcom hiked prices 175% on already-purchased perpetual licenses. UK High Court case. Tesco calls it "abusive conduct."

Tesco bought perpetual VMware licenses + support through 2026 in 2021. Broadcom refused to honor it post-acquisition. They offered VCF 9.0 at 3.5M/yr — 175% VMware hike, 350% mainframe hike. Tesco turned down 4+ offers.

Migration target: end of 2027 at "exceptional pace." Incompatible backup tools (Veeam, Zerto). Third-party support since Broadcom cut them off Jan 2026. Court date: late 2027-early 2028.

AT&T sued over 1050% hike (settled). Siemens in active litigation. HPE and Nutanix aggressively poaching. The window to negotiate VMware exit is now.
