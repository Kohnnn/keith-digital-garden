# Apple WWDC 2026: Siri AI, Gemini, and the Cook Farewell

WWDC 2026 was Apple's most consequential keynote in a decade. Siri rebuilt on Google's Gemini. iOS 27. Tim Cook's emotional farewell. Apple stopped making promises and started delivering.

---

## Siri AI: Built on Gemini

The headline: Siri is now Siri AI, powered by Google's Gemini. After years of playing catch-up, Apple licensed Gemini as the foundation model layer while keeping its own Apple Foundation Models for on-device processing. The hybrid architecture: simple requests handled by AFM on-device (private, fast), complex reasoning routed to Gemini in Private Cloud Compute.

Key features: voice customization (pick your Siri voice personality), on-screen awareness (Siri sees what you're looking at), deep personal context (across apps, messages, calendar, health data), and agentic actions (book flights, edit photos, send money — all by voice).

Privacy architecture: Apple's Private Cloud Compute ensures data never leaves Apple's infrastructure. Gemini handles only anonymized reasoning, not raw personal data. Apple Intelligence remains the privacy layer.

## iOS 27

The biggest iOS update in years: AI-powered notification triage, live transcripts for all audio, Visual Intelligence camera (point at anything to get AI analysis), redesigned lock screen with live AI widgets. The Gemini integration is deep — Siri AI replaces search, suggests replies, and proactively surfaces information.

## Tim Cook's Farewell

Cook became visibly emotional during the keynote. After 15 years as CEO, he's stepping down later this year. The WWDC keynote was his final major product launch. Industry speculation points to Jeff Williams or John Ternus as successor.

## The Strategic Shift

The Gemini deal is Apple admitting it can't win the model war. But that's fine — Apple's moat is distribution, privacy infrastructure, and user trust. By licensing the best model and wrapping it in Private Cloud Compute, Apple gets AI capability without compromising its brand. The strategy: own the UX, outsource the model.
