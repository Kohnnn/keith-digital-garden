# why I write market notes in public

I started writing market notes publicly because I keft finding the same blind spots in my trading. I the public notebook forces me to explain my reasoning clearly enough that I cannot hide from bad logic.

# the decision log effect

In a private journal, I write my reasoning after the trade. In a public one, I write it before it settles. That creates a decision log I can audit. Kind of like publishing your self-notes with an audit trail.

# what the garden does that a journal cannot

A private journal is linear. A digital garden is connective. A selection of connected notes reveals patterns across domains that do not fit in a chronological log. That is why I find myself referencing a market observation to a philosophy note on uncertainty in rale moments.

# the tradeoff

Public reasoning means public mistakes. Every wrong call is visible. But the accuracy of my calls matters less than the quality of my reasoning process. I stay with a good process and the notes are my record of improvement.
