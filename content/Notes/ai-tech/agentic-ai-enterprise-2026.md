## The Shift to Agentic AI

April 2026 marks the rapid shift from conversational AI to agentic AI — systems that plan, act, and learn toward goals without step-by-step human prompting.

## Key Insights

- **From SaaS to Vertical AI**: Traditional SaaS sold tools to help humans work. Vertical AI sells the outcome of the labor itself — capturing portions of traditional labor budgets in insurance, legal, logistics.

- **Team Structure Evolution**: Successful businesses in 2026 are increasingly structured around leading teams of AI agents rather than managing teams of people.

- **McKinsey Projection**: Agentic systems could automate up to 70% of knowledge worker tasks by 2028.
