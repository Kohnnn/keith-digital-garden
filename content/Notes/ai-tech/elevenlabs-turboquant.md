## ElevenLabs — Image and Video (Beta)

ElevenLabs launched Image and Video in beta, expanding from audio-first to unified creative platform.

**Capabilities:**
- Generate images and videos using Veo, Sora, Kling, WAN, Seedance
- Bring to life with ElevenLabs voices, music, sound effects
- Lipsync for generated videos
- Composition timeline for multi-clip storytelling
- Direct export to ElevenLabs Studio

**Impact:** ElevenLabs is now a direct competitor to standalone video generation tools — positioned as end-to-end creative production platform.

## Google Research — TurboQuant

Google Research introduced **TurboQuant**, a KV cache compression algorithm.

**Key Claims:**
- Reduces AI inference memory requirements by **6x**
- No accuracy loss on benchmarks
- Targets KV cache — primary bottleneck for long context windows

**Market Impact:**
| Company | Stock Drop |
|---------|-----------|
| SK Hynix | -6% |
| Samsung | -5% |
| Micron | -2% |

Investors repriced long-term demand outlook for AI memory chips.

**For developers:**
- Lower infrastructure costs
- Faster adoption of long-context models
- Wider hardware range support
