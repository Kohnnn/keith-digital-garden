## Overview

**Nous Research** is a leader in the American open source AI movement. They train world-class open source language models and build infrastructure to coordinate distributed, unbiased training.

Their mission: advance human rights and freedoms by creating and proliferating open source language models, supporting their unrestricted availability and use.

## Key Projects

### Hermes Agent (95k stars)
"The agent that grows with you" — A general-purpose AI agent with skill-based architecture.

- 95,592 GitHub stars
- MIT licensed
- Supports skill loading, MCP tools, multi-turn conversations
- Built-in environments and plugins

### DisTrO
Distributed Training Over-The-Internet — Training language models across distributed nodes.

### Atropos
Language Model Reinforcement Learning Environments framework for collecting and evaluating LLM trajectories through diverse environments.

### Autoreason
Autoresearch for subjective domains — exploring reasoning in ambiguous contexts.

### Hermes-Agent-Self-Evolution
Evolutionary self-improvement for Hermes Agent — optimize skills, prompts, and code using DSPy + GEPA.

## Research Focus

- **Model architecture**: Novel transformer designs and efficiency improvements
- **Data synthesis**: Generating high-quality training data
- **Fine-tuning**: Adapting models to specific domains
- **Reasoning**: Chain-of-thought, tool use, multi-step problem solving
