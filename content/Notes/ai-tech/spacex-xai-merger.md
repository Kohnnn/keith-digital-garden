## The Largest Merger in History

On February 2, 2026, Elon Musk announced SpaceX acquired xAI in a deal valued at $250 billion — creating a combined entity worth over $1.25 trillion.

**Deal Structure:**
- xAI Valuation: $250 billion
- SpaceX Internal Valuation: $1 trillion
- Combined Entity: $1.25 trillion
- Expected IPO: Mid-2026 (potentially raising $50B)

## Strategic Logic

SpaceX's Starlink satellite broadband has delivered strong revenue growth, supporting valuation lift from ~$800B to $1T. The acquisition folds xAI into SpaceX's ecosystem: space, satellites, and X/Twitter social media.

Musk's vision: "the most ambitious, vertically-integrated innovation engine on (and off) Earth."

## Q1 2026: Record Venture Funding

The funding data is staggering:
- Global startup investment: $297B in Q1 2026 (150% increase YoY)
- AI startups: $242B (81% of all venture capital)

**Top 5 Largest Venture Rounds (All Q1 2026):**

These 4 rounds exceeded total global venture funding of all 2024.
