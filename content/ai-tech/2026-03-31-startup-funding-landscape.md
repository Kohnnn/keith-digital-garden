# Startup Funding Landscape: AI Boom and Market Correction

The 2024-2026 period saw dramatic shifts in startup funding, with AI capturing unprecedented capital while other sectors faced correction.

## Funding Environment Overview

### Total VC Investment Trends

| Year | Global VC | AI-Specific | AI % of Total |
|------|-----------|-------------|---------------|
| 2021 | $295B | $45B | 15% |
| 2022 | $168B | $52B | 31% |
| 2023 | $95B | $48B | 51% |
| 2024 | $142B | $92B | 65% |
| 2025 | $178B | $128B | 72% |

## Market Correction Impact

### Non-AI Sectors Facing Headwinds

| Sector | 2024→2025 Change | Key Factors |
|--------|-------------------|-------------|
| Consumer | -45% | Rising rates, pullback |
| Crypto | -60% | Bear market |
| PropTech | -55% | Real estate crash |
| EdTech | -40% | Post-COVID pullback |
| HealthTech | -35% | Reimbursement pressure |

### Survival Characteristics

Companies that thrived despite correction:

1. **Path to profitability**: Burn multiple under 18 months
2. **Clear use case**: Revenue, not just users
3. **Defensible moat**: Proprietary data, network effects
4. **Efficient growth**: Less than 1x burn multiple

## Founder Perspectives

### What Changed Post-2022

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| "Grow at all costs" | "Efficiency matters" |
| "We'll figure out monetization" | "Revenue day one" |
| "Series A with 10M users" | "Series A with $1M ARR" |
| "VC cash is cheap" | "Dilution is expensive" |

### AI Startup Realities

HN-founder insights:

> "We're burning $2M/month but growing 20% month-over-month. Investors still want path to profitability."

> "The compute costs for our LLM product are 40% of revenue. Need to optimize or die."

> "Enterprise sales cycles haven't shortened. Still 6-12 months for $100K+ deals."

## Emerging Opportunities

### Underexplored AI Niches

| Area | Opportunity | Competition Level |
|------|-------------|-------------------|
| AI for regulatory compliance | High | Medium |
| Vertical AI (legal, medical, finance) | Very High | Medium |
| AI debugging/profiling tools | Medium | Low |
| AI governance/ethics tooling | High | Low |
| Edge AI / on-device | Medium | Medium |

## 2026 Outlook

Key themes for the year:
- Foundation model consolidation (fewer winners)
- Vertical AI success stories emerging
- AI infrastructure commoditization
- Governance/regulation creating new markets
