# W06 btc consolidates near 72k as altcoins digest january gains

Bitcoin found its footing in the $71-74K range after January's Fed-driven volatility. The week's action was orderly consolidation, with buyers defending $71K and sellers capping gains at $74K. Source: [TradingView - BTC/USD](https://www.tradingview.com/)

## scene cut

The Fed held rates steady while January jobs smashed expectations, forcing a repricing of rate cut odds from May to June—but Bitcoin's $71K support held firm through the uncertainty.

## signal braid

- Bitcoin ETF inflows recovered to $142M weekly, a positive sign after January's outflow trend; BlackRock's IBIT led with $89M
- Ethereum reclaimed $4K on EIP-4844 testnet anticipation, with ETH/BTC holding 0.0555
- Solana DeFi hit $12B TVL for the first time since May 2025, driven by Jupiter DEX's $8.2B weekly volume

## my take

I think this week set the foundation for W08's breakout. The Fed uncertainty was noise—ETF flows stabilizing and ETH reclaiming $4K told me institutional conviction survived January's volatility. Solana's DeFi momentum is the under-the-radar story here.
