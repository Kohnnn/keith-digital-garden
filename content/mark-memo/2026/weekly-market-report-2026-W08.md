## Key Themes

### Fed Minutes Reveal Internal Division

The February 19 release of January 28-29 FOMC meeting minutes sent shockwaves through markets. While the committee voted unanimously to hold rates at 5.25%-5.50%, the minutes revealed significant debate about the timing of future cuts. Three members expressed concern that maintaining restrictive policy "too long" risked unnecessary economic damage, while two hawks argued inflation remained "insufficiently contained" to consider easing.

Most significantly, the minutes included a novel phrase: policymakers would monitor "realized labor market softening" rather than requiring "further improvement." Markets interpreted this as a pivot toward data dependency that could open door to cuts as early as May 2026.
