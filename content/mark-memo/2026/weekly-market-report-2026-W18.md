# weekly market report 2026-w18 btc reclaims 80k as project freedom eases hormuz risk

Bitcoin pushed back toward $79K into Sunday's close and tagged $80K in early Asian hours after Trump unveiled Project Freedom — a US Navy operation escorting civilian ships through the Strait of Hormuz. Total crypto market cap rose 1.88% to $2.63T. April delivered BTC's best monthly performance in 12 months at +11.87% ([YEX](https://blog.yex.io/markets/market-recap-markets/crypto-market-weekly-recap-may-4-2026/)).

## scene cut
The Bitcoin 2026 Conference in Las Vegas dominated the narrative this week. I watched from the sidelines — conferences create noise, not signal. The real story was the institutional pipeline and what Trump's Project Freedom means for risk appetite.

- BTC touched $80K for the first time since February on Project Freedom announcement

## my take
The $80K reclaim matters because it happened on a geopolitical catalyst that actually reduced risk (Hormuz de-escalation) rather than speculative froth. $1.97B in monthly ETF inflows is institutional — not retail. I'm cautiously constructive but want to see $80K hold as support before adding size.
