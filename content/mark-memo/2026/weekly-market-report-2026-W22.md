# weekly market report 2026-w22 btc tests 70k support as macro toll mounts

Bitcoin wrapped May sealing a roughly 3% monthly loss, wedged under its 2025 yearly lows before clawing back toward $73,600 into the monthly close. The market shed over $300B in value during the final week, with total cap slipping toward $2.5T. BTC dominance held near 58.5% — capital hiding in the majors ([YEX](https://blog.yex.io/markets/market-recap-markets/crypto-market-weekly-recap-june-1-2026/)).

*see also:* [[weekly market report 2026-w21 twin inflation prints strain btc as institutional conviction tested]] · [[weekly market report 2026-w23 btc bounces above 70k as market divergence deepens]]

## signal braid
- BTC tested $71.4K support before bouncing to $73.6K late in the week
- Spot BTC ETFs logged 10th straight outflow day — $2.97B in net redemptions since May 15
- ETH ETFs extended own outflow streak past 10 days — $216M over 7 sessions
- Fear & Greed Index hit 23 ("Extreme Fear") — yet social sentiment showed 2.23 bullish per bearish (most lopsided of 2026)
- US stocks notched fresh ATHs on Iran ceasefire hopes — BTC failed to catch the tailwind
- US Treasury seized ~$1B in Iranian crypto under Operation Economic Fury
- Brent crude eased on ceasefire optimism; oil entered correction territory
- Stellar (XLM), Monero (XMR), Zcash (ZEC) outperformed as risk rotated to pockets

## my take
The divergence between BTC and equities is the story. Equities making ATHs on AI euphoria while crypto is in Extreme Fear with 10-day ETF outflows. That gap doesn't close quietly — either crypto catches up, or equities correct. The social sentiment divergence (Extreme Fear price vs bullish social reads) historically resolves downward. I'm cautious here and waiting for either an ETF flow reversal or a sustained reclaim of $75K before adding risk.
