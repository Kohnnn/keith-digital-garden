# weekly market report 2026-w23 btc bounces above 70k as market divergence deepens

Bitcoin started the week circling $71.4K support before bouncing toward $73.6K, still wedged under the 2025 yearly lows. The market shed over $300B in May's final week — total cap at $2.5T. All eyes now on a packed US jobs week that could decide which way the market breaks ([YEX](https://blog.yex.io/markets/market-recap-markets/crypto-market-weekly-recap-june-1-2026/)).

## scene cut
The bounces are getting weaker, the ranges are getting narrower, and the ETF outflows are getting longer. I'm treating this as base-building under duress, not a reversal pattern. The jobs data this week is the real catalyst — a hot print and we sweep the lows.

- BTC holding $71.4K-$73.6K range with declining volatility

## my take
The Vietnam news is the kind of slow-build structural catalyst that matters more than any single week's price. A sovereign recognizing crypto as legitimate collateral for 98% of its businesses is not a nothing headline. But in the near term, the ETF outflow streak and Extreme Fear reading dominate. I'm watching this week's jobs data as the trigger — a miss could spark a relief rally, a hot print could take us to $68K. Positioned neutral, ready either way.
