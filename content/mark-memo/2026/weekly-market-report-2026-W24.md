# weekly market report 2026-w24 payrolls week as btc consolidation and equity divergence test resolve

Bitcoin spent the first week of June consolidating the $71-75K zone as the divergence with equities deepened. The S&P 500 closed at 7,580 — nine consecutive winning weeks, the longest streak since December 2023. The Nasdaq pushed May gains beyond 8%. Meanwhile global crypto ETP outflows deepened to $1.5B, with US spot BTC ETFs seeing ~$1.42B in outflows and Bitcoin products posting their worst weekly redemptions of 2026 (Zerocap).

## scene cut
The divergence is now extreme. Equities have notched nine straight up weeks while crypto ETFs bleed $1.4B in a single week. I am watching Friday's NFP print as the trigger. A soft print could reverse sentiment. A hot print likely takes BTC toward $68K.

## my take
Nine weeks of equity gains while crypto bleeds is not sustainable. Something breaks. The NFP data Friday is the most important macro input of the month. If the labor market is softening, rate expectations ease, and crypto catches a relief bid. If payrolls print hot, the Warsh no-cut stance strengthens and BTC likely sweeps $68K. I am neutral position with a slight lean toward the soft print — consumer confidence data has been deteriorating for months and the labor market usually lags.
