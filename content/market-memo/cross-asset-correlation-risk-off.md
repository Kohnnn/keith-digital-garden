# Cross-Asset Correlation Breakdown in Risk-Off Environments

Traditional diversification breaks down when you need it most — or does it? The 2025-2026 period has challenged conventional wisdom about correlation in stress.

## The 60/40 Problem Revisited

Bonds and equities have decoupled:
- In 2025, 60/40 portfolios outperformed pure equity by 8%
- Bond-equity correlation flipped briefly negative in Q3
- Duration risk reasserted itself in rate volatile periods

## Crypto Behavior Shift

Bitcoin has matured as a macro asset:

| Asset | 2020 Bear | 2022 Bear | 2025 Bear |
|-------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| S&P 500 | -25% | -25% | -12% |
| BTC | -60% | -65% | -35% |
| Gold | +5% | -20% | +8% |
| Bonds | -10% | -15% | +5% |

Crypto still leads volatility, but the beta has moderated.

## Practical Implications

### True Diversifiers Now
Gold, short-duration treasuries, and quality FX (JPY, CHF) remain reliable risk-off hedges.

### Crypto Allocation
Consider BTC as a "risk-on/risk-off" rather than pure risk asset. Position sizing matters more than direction.

### Regime Detection
Monitor:
- TED spread (credit stress)
- Junk bond spreads
- VIX term structure

These give early warning before correlation breakdown.

---

*Key takeaway: Diversification works again — but not all assets. Rebalance toward true hedges in uncertain regimes.*
