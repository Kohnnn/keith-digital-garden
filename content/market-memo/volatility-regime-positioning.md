# Volatility Regime Changes and Positioning Implications

The VIX has settled into a new paradigm — lower baseline with sharper, shorter spikes. Understanding this regime shift is critical for positioning.

## The New Normal

Post-2024, volatility patterns have fundamentally changed:

| Metric | 2020-2023 | 2024-2026 |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| VIX average | 22 | 14 |
| VIX spikes (>30) | Frequent | Rare, sharp |
| Recovery time | Weeks | Days |
| Correlation | High | Moderate |

## Drivers

### Market Resilience
Institutional cash on sidelines ($6T+ in money markets) provides steady buy-the-dip liquidity. Each dip gets absorbed faster.

### Options Market Maturation
Volatility products and retail options participation have created more sophisticated hedging, dampening sustained moves.

### Macro Stabilization
Fed communication has become more predictable. "Fed put" pricing is well understood, reducing tail risk premium.

## Positioning Implications

### Short Vol Strategies
Premium selling works — but size accordingly. The occasional spike will hurt if overleveraged.

### Long Gamma Fans
Stay patient. Opportunities come from unexpected catalysts, not routine market stress.

### Correlation Trades
Diversification actually works again. The "everything correlated in crisis" dynamic has softened.

---

*Key takeaway: Lower vol baseline = premium selling works. But size for the occasional sharp spike.*
