The CFTC has filed lawsuits against Arizona, Connecticut, and Illinois, arguing that federal law—not state gambling rules—governs platforms like Kalshi and Polymarket.

**The legal battle:**

- Critical for the future of crypto-native prediction markets

**Features:**
