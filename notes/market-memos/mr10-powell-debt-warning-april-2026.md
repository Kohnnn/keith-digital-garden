## 💰 The Debt Warning

Powell identified **spending trajectory** as the real problem—not the current $39 trillion debt level itself:

**The core issue:**
- Congress continues spending more than the U.S. takes in
- Current path described as "not sustainable"
- Economic growth must outpace spending growth

**Historical context:**
- Fed achieved "soft landing" in late 2024
- 12-month inflation just above 2%
- Labor market essentially at full employment
- But structural fiscal challenges remain

## ⚖️ Fed's Approach to Iran War Impact

Powell outlined the Fed's **"wait-and-see"** approach:

| Factor | Fed Response |
|--------|--------------|
| Oil price spike | Monitor carefully |
| Inflation expectations | Watch "very, very carefully" |
| Policy adjustments | Wait for sustained impact |

**Historical precedent:** Fed tends not to react to oil/gas price rises because energy supply shocks are often short-lived. This time may be different—but they're not jumping to conclusions.

## 🏦 Financial System Resilience

Powell expressed confidence in the U.S. financial system's resilience:

**Fortification measures:**
- Significant measures taken against outsized risks
- Credit loss safeguards strengthened
- Lessons from 2008 Global Financial Crisis applied

**Private credit monitoring:**
- FOMC monitoring private credit markets "super carefully"
- No systemic threat currently identified
- But constant vigilance required

> "What the country needs from the Fed is 'vigilance,' not the elimination of all risk. You just need to always know that there's another thing coming."

## 🎓 Fed Independence Defense

Powell made a strong case for Fed independence:

> "We're not trying to work against any politician or any administration, but we have to be careful to stick to what we're doing."

**His position:**
- Fed is "not a perfect institution"
- Policymaking is "highly uncertain"
- But it remains "a great American institution"

**Warning:** Any interference with Fed independence would "threaten significant harm to the financial system."

> "It's very hard to build great democratic institutions and much easier to bring them down."
